Chun-Yen Wu吳俊彥

Assistant Research Fellow Second (International) Research Division · Chung-Hua Institution for Economic Research (CIER)

I am an economist working on international trade, with research spanning the trade–labor market nexus and applied microeconomics. My recent work examines dominant-currency pricing, the effects of trade policy on firms, and the economics of carbon pricing. I use rich firm-level data to understand how firms and workers respond to a changing global trading environment.
